## Summary

Go Flashcards does not collect, transmit or share your personal information. Everything you create or import stays
on your device.

## What the app stores, and where

The app saves the following **on your device only**:

- the puzzles you import or create (the SGF text and its title)
- your practice progress: attempt history, skill levels, spaced-repetition and guided-learning data, mastery scores,
  and a rolling set of up to 15 backups of those scores
- your settings

This data is kept in the app's local storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no account, no
sign-in and no server.

## Files you choose

When you import puzzles, you pick files from your device (or paste text). The app reads only the files you select,
only on your device, and only to add the puzzles in them to your library.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect analytics or usage statistics.
- It does not show advertising and does not use advertising identifiers.
- It does not track you across other apps or websites.
- It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or notifications.
- It does not make network requests to send your data anywhere.

## Backups made by your device

Your phone or tablet may include the app's data in its own device backup (for example an iCloud or Google backup),
depending on your device settings. That is controlled by your operating system and your account with Apple or
Google, not by this app.

## Deleting your data

Removing a puzzle or collection from within the app deletes it from the app's storage. Uninstalling the app deletes
all of its data from your device.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. It is not directed at children
under 13 and contains no social, chat or purchase features.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
